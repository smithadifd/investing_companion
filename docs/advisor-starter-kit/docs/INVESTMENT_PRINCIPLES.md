## Research & Decision Framework *(reusable as-is)*

### Before entering a position
1. **What's the thesis?** Can I explain it simply?
2. **What's the upside?** Realistic, not hopium.
3. **What's the downside?** What could go wrong?
4. **What would invalidate the thesis?** Know the exit criteria.
5. **What's the time horizon?** When should this play out?
6. **Position size?** Appropriate for conviction and risk.

### During the position
- Monitor key metrics, not daily prices.
- Reassess when new information emerges.
- Don't anchor to entry price.
- Let winners run; cut losers (easier said than done).

### Exiting
- Thesis achieved · thesis invalidated · better opportunity elsewhere · sizing discipline
  (trim winners that grow too large).

## Risk Management *(reusable defaults)*

- **Portfolio level:** diversify across *themes*, not just tickers; some dry powder for
  opportunities; emergency fund kept separate from the portfolio.
- **Position level:** know the thesis and invalidation; size for uncertainty; be willing to be
  wrong and move on.
- **Behavioral:** don't chase (FOMO is expensive) — *but* distinguish chasing momentum
  exhaustion from buying into a structural re-rating with aligned technicals. Don't panic (fear
  is expensive too). Process over outcomes.
