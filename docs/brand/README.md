# Brand mark

`mark.svg` is the investing companion mark: a solid blue disc with one price line cut through
it. The line has sharp corners and uneven rises and falls, like a day's chart, and ends about
level with where it began. It shows the market moving without promising a direction.

- Accent: `#3B82F6` (Tailwind `blue-500`), the only colour in the mark. The line is negative
  space, so the background shows through it.
- A smooth, even wave is avoided on purpose: it reads as sound, not price.
- No handle. With a handle the circle becomes a magnifying glass, which reads as search.
- Blue rather than green, because green already means gain inside the app.
- The SVG has a transparent background. The app's dark pages use `neutral-900` (`#171717`).

Checked at 16, 32 and 128 px on `#171717` and on white. The line still reads at 16 px on both.

Drawn by hand as SVG; no image model was used.
